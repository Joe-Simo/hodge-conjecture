# U07: exact matrix-factorization target for the degree-70 Fermat orbit

Status: verified target reduction for one concrete Hodge eigenspace only. This does not prove its algebraicity and does not solve the Hodge conjecture.

Date: 2026-09-14.

## 1. From character to Jacobian monomial

For the Fermat fourfold

  X = {Q=0} subset P^5,
  Q=x0^70+x1^70+x2^70+x3^70+x4^70+x5^70,

the Jacobian ring is

  Jac(Q)=C[x0,...,x5]/(x0^69,...,x5^69).

Favero-Kelly Proposition 4.2 associates to a character

  alpha=(a0,...,a5)

the monomial

  M(alpha)=x0^(a0-1)...x5^(a5-1).

For

  alpha=(1,20,24,42,61,62),

we obtain

  M_alpha = x1^19 x2^23 x3^41 x4^60 x5^61.

Its total degree is

  0+19+23+41+60+61 = 204.

For a fourfold k=2, the primitive (2,2) Jacobian degree is

  (k+1)m-(2k+2) = 3*70-6 = 204,

so M_alpha lies in exactly the required graded piece.

All exponents are <=68, so it survives in Jac(Q).

## 2. Galois orbit certificate

For every unit u mod 70, form the least-positive-residue character u*alpha and subtract 1 coordinatewise. Exact Wolfram arithmetic gives:

- 24 distinct unit-orbit characters;
- every corresponding exponent vector has total degree 204;
- every exponent is in [0,68].

Thus the entire rational Hodge block is represented by 24 one-dimensional complex eigenspaces inside the correct primitive Jacobian piece.

## 3. One nonzero coefficient is enough

Favero-Kelly Proposition 4.4 is crucial. If a coherent sheaf F on X has

  ch_2^prim(F) = sum_M c_M M

in Jac(Q), and c_(M_alpha) != 0, then character orthogonality over the Fermat symmetry group projects algebraically onto the eigenspace V(alpha).

Therefore it is NOT necessary to construct a sheaf whose primitive Chern character equals M_alpha exactly.

It is enough to construct any coherent sheaf (or algebraic cycle through its structure sheaf) whose primitive Chern character contains M_alpha with a nonzero coefficient.

This is a much weaker and more executable target.

## 4. Matrix-factorization certificate

Favero-Kelly Theorem 3.6 gives, for a matrix factorization A,B of Q,

  AB=BA=Q id,

a trace polynomial T(A,B) in Jac(Q) representing ch_2^prim(coker A), up to an explicit nonzero scalar.

Hence a concrete algebraicity certificate for V(alpha) is:

  find finite square matrices A,B over C[x0,...,x5]
  with AB=BA=Q id
  such that coefficient_(M_alpha)(T(A,B)) != 0.

Once such A,B are found, Proposition 4.4 isolates V(alpha) by averaging under the finite diagonal Fermat symmetry group.

This finite certificate would establish algebraicity of the complex eigenspace V(alpha); rationality of the full orbit then follows by taking the Galois/rational span of the resulting algebraic classes.

## 5. Why standard complete intersections do not immediately solve it

Favero-Kelly Example 4.5 recovers the classical paired complete intersections. Their characters have the form

  (l0,70-l0,l1,70-l1,l2,70-l2)

up to permutation.

U06 checked that alpha contains no pair summing to 0 modulo 70. Therefore alpha cannot arise from this standard paired complete-intersection construction.

Their degree-33 breakthrough is instructive: the previously elusive character was reached by a Shioda rational map from a special cubic fourfold whose (2,2) lattice is generated by cubic scrolls, rather than by a new complete intersection on the Fermat fourfold itself.

This suggests two concrete construction classes for degree 70:

1. a genuinely non-Koszul higher-rank matrix factorization on X_70;
2. a Shioda/Delsarte rational map from X_70 to a lower-degree fourfold with a known algebraic Hodge lattice, followed by pullback of an algebraic surface.

Neither construction has yet been produced.

## 6. Exact success criterion for the next experiment

Any proposed factorization/cycle should be rejected unless its exact Favero-Kelly trace polynomial is computed in Jac(Q) and the coefficient of

  x1^19 x2^23 x3^41 x4^60 x5^61

(or one of its 23 Galois conjugates) is proved nonzero.

A bounded search that fails to find such a factorization is not evidence of non-algebraicity.
