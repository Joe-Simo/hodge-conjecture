# U01: exact fourfold reduction to primitive rational (2,2)-classes

Status: verified partial reduction only. This does not solve the Hodge conjecture.

Date: 2026-09-14.

Let X be a smooth projective complex fourfold and h in H^2(X,Q) the hyperplane class. Write L(gamma)=h cup gamma. The only genuinely open codimension for a fourfold is p=2, so let alpha in H^4(X,Q) cap H^{2,2}(X).

Hard Lefschetz gives an isomorphism L^2:H^2(X,Q)->H^6(X,Q). Define beta=(L^2)^(-1)(L alpha) and alpha_prim=alpha-L beta. Then L alpha_prim=0. The decomposition is unique because L^2 is injective. Since L is rational and of Hodge type (1,1), beta is a rational (1,1)-class and alpha_prim is a rational (2,2)-class.

By the Lefschetz (1,1) theorem, beta is a rational linear combination of divisor classes, so L beta is a rational linear combination of codimension-2 algebraic cycle classes. Therefore on smooth projective fourfolds the rational Hodge conjecture is equivalent to algebraicity of all primitive rational (2,2)-classes in H^4.

## Why naive hyperplane induction fails

Let i:Y->X be a smooth hyperplane section. For primitive alpha, the projection formula gives i_*i^*(alpha)=h cup alpha=0. The restricted class i^*(alpha) is a rational (2,2)-class on the smooth projective threefold Y, hence algebraic by the dimension-3 theorem, but a codimension-2 cycle on Y has codimension 3 inside X and its Gysin pushforward represents 0 rather than alpha. Thus hyperplane-section induction loses exactly the primitive class and needs additional correspondence/normal-function input.

## Exact toy model: P^2 x P^2

Let a,b be the two hyperplane classes with a^3=b^3=0 and h=a+b. In bases {a^2,ab,b^2} of H^4 and {a^2b,ab^2} of H^6, multiplication by h has matrix [[1,1,0],[0,1,1]]. Exact Wolfram Language computation gives rank 2 and nullspace generated by (1,-1,1), so primitive H^4 is generated by a^2-ab+b^2. It is algebraic because every term is an intersection of divisors.

The universal frontier is therefore the primitive rational (2,2) part of H^4 on smooth projective fourfolds. A proof must construct codimension-2 cycles there without importing an unproved algebraic correspondence; a counterexample must prove a primitive class lies outside the span of every codimension-2 algebraic cycle class.
