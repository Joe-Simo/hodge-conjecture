# U19: corrected power-map pullback family for the degree-70 target

Status: verified character-theoretic correction and exact finite checks. This does not prove algebraicity and does not solve the Hodge conjecture.

Date: 2026-09-14.

## Correction to the level-lift search

For the coordinatewise power map

    pi_k : X_{70k}^4 -> X_70^4,
    [x_i] |-> [x_i^k],

the pullback of the degree-70 character

    alpha=(1,20,24,42,61,62)

is not an arbitrary coordinate lift congruent to alpha modulo 70. The natural pullback character is

    beta_k = k alpha = (k,20k,24k,42k,61k,62k) mod 70k.

This follows directly from the diagonal action: a level-70 diagonal root zeta_70^{a_i} becomes zeta_{70k}^{k a_i} upstairs.

Therefore U16-U17's arbitrary-lift searches are exploratory arithmetic only and must not be interpreted as the actual pullback family.

## Rational Hodge condition is automatically preserved

For a unit u modulo 70k, u is also coprime to 70. Since

    <u (k a_i)>_{70k} = k <u a_i>_70,

we have

    sum_i <u beta_{k,i}>_{70k}
      = k sum_i <u alpha_i>_70
      = k*(3*70)
      = 3*(70k).

Thus every beta_k is a rational (2,2) Hodge character whenever alpha is.

Exact Wolfram checks for every k=2,...,20 confirm this identity.

## Elementary decomposability does not improve under pure scaling

A triple of beta_k sums to zero modulo 70k exactly when the corresponding triple of alpha sums to zero modulo 70, because

    70k | k*S  iff  70 | S.

U06 established that alpha has no zero-sum triple partition. Therefore no pure power-map pullback beta_k can acquire the elementary two-zero-sum-triples decomposition at any k.

Exact Wolfram checks for k=2,...,20 confirm no such decomposition.

The same scaling invariance applies to complementary pairs:

    k(a_i+a_j) == 0 mod 70k

iff

    a_i+a_j == 0 mod 70.

So pure level multiplication cannot repair the binary-factor obstruction either.

## Consequence

Searching larger k for ordinary pair/triple decomposability of beta_k is mathematically redundant. The obstruction scales exactly with the level.

A useful level-lift route must therefore introduce something beyond pure pullback, such as:

- auxiliary cancelling coordinates followed by a geometric join/projection;
- a nontrivial Delsarte map whose exponent matrix mixes coordinates;
- an algebraic cycle upstairs that is not generated by the same pair/triple combinatorics and whose pushforward is nonzero downstairs.

This substantially narrows future level-change searches and prevents wasting computation on scaled copies of the same obstruction.
