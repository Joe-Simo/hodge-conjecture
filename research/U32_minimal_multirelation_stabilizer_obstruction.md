# U32: stabilizer obstruction for the two minimal multi-monomial relations

Status: exact character-theoretic obstruction for the specific U31 two-relation surface ansatz. This does not rule out other multi-monomial cycles and does not solve the Hodge conjecture.

Date: 2026-09-14.

## Candidate

U31 found the two primitive support-3 relations

    rA=(0,-1,3,0,-2,0),
    rB=(0,-4,1,3,0,0)

for alpha=(1,20,24,42,61,62) modulo 70.

On the dense torus of the Fermat cover, a surface cut out by the two corresponding Laurent equations has diagonal stabilizer consisting of t in (Z/70)^6 satisfying

    rA dot t = 0 mod 70,
    rB dot t = 0 mod 70,

modulo the projective diagonal.

For the alpha-eigenprojection of such a stabilized cycle to be nonzero, alpha must be trivial on the stabilizer. Equivalently alpha must lie in the mod-70 span of rA, rB and the projective diagonal vector 1=(1,1,1,1,1,1).

## Exact test

Exhaustive exact arithmetic over coefficients a,b,c in Z/70 finds no solution of

    alpha == a rA + b rB + c 1  (mod 70).

An explicit stabilizer element witnessing nontriviality is

    t=(0,0,0,0,35,0).

Indeed

    rA dot t = -70 == 0 mod 70,
    rB dot t = 0,

but

    alpha dot t = 61*35 == 35 mod 70 != 0.

Therefore alpha is nontrivial on the stabilizer.

## Consequence

Any cycle class invariant under this full two-relation stabilizer has zero projection to V(alpha). Thus the most economical surface obtained by imposing exactly the two U31 support-3 relations cannot represent the target Hodge eigenspace.

This is a clean failure of the minimal multi-relation ansatz, not of all toric or quotient-side surfaces. A successful relation-defined surface must use a relation lattice L whose annihilator is contained in ker(alpha), equivalently alpha lies in L + <1> modulo 70.

## New search criterion

Instead of searching individual small relations and combining them ad hoc, search rank-3 relation lattices L generated by small primitive vectors satisfying homogeneity, and require from the outset

    alpha in rowspan_mod70(L, 1).

Rank 3 is natural for a codimension-3 torus construction before accounting for the hypersurface relation. This stabilizer criterion is much stronger and cheaper than constructing the corresponding varieties first.
