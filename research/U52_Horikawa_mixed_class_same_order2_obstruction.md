# U52: the Horikawa mixed class is exactly the same order-two motivic obstruction as the original Fermat target

**Status:** exact relation-lattice identification, with a complete Smith-normal-form calculation and a simple parity witness. This does not construct the missing correspondence and does not solve the Hodge conjecture.

Date: 2026-09-16.

## 1. Characters from U46

The two rank-24 surface pieces are represented on the degree-70 Fermat surface by

    beta  = (1,42,62,35),
    gamma = (20,24,61,35).

The mixed Hodge class in `R5 tensor R7` corresponds, under Poincare duality, to a morphism from the dual of the beta line to the gamma line. Thus the relevant motivic comparison is

    M_{-beta} -> M_gamma,

not `M_beta -> M_gamma`.

This sign is load-bearing.

## 2. Formal Gauss-word lattice

Use the same 70-coordinate lattice as U42-U43. Coordinates 1,...,69 are the formal Gauss symbols e_a and coordinate 70 is the Lefschetz/q symbol ell.

Let L be the integer lattice generated by

- the 35 reflection relations

      R(a)=e_a+e_{70-a}-ell,  1<=a<=35,

- every level-70 Davenport-Hasse multiplication relation for divisors n=2,5,7 in the U42 convention.

There are 91 generators. The exact Smith normal form of the 70x91 generator matrix has

    56 invariant factors equal to 1,
    one invariant factor equal to 2,
    13 zero rows.

Hence the torsion subgroup of the quotient is exactly Z/2.

## 3. The mixed class has exact order two

Define the mixed comparison word

    Delta = sum_{a in gamma} e_a - sum_{a in -beta} e_a.

(The normalized q/Lefschetz powers cancel because both surface motives have weight 2.)

The Smith calculation gives:

1. Delta has zero component in all 13 free quotient directions;
2. its coordinate in the unique torsion direction is odd;
3. therefore Delta is not in L but 2 Delta is in L.

Thus Delta is the nonzero generator of the unique Z/2 torsion subgroup.

An explicit 35-term integral certificate for `2 Delta` is obtained from the same reflection/Davenport-Hasse generator system; its coefficient list is the one returned by the exact Smith solve and agrees with the squared Jacobi-sum certificate already appearing in the arithmetic route.

## 4. Exact identification with the original fourfold target

Let

    T_alpha = e_1+e_20+e_24+e_42+e_61+e_62 - 3 ell

be the normalized original target word.

Exact integer arithmetic gives the remarkably short identity

    Delta - T_alpha = -R(1)-R(8)-R(28).

Therefore

    [Delta] = [T_alpha] in Z^70/L.

So the missing mixed-product correspondence of U46-U48 and the original degree-70 Fermat Hodge class are literally the same obstruction in the classical motivic relation quotient, not merely two problems with similar Hodge numbers.

## 5. Four-coordinate parity witness

The U42 parity functional

    epsilon(v)=v_14+v_28+v_42+v_56 mod 2

annihilates every reflection and every admissible level-70 Davenport-Hasse multiplication relation.

For Delta one has

    epsilon(Delta)=1.

Indeed gamma contains none of {14,28,42,56}, whereas -beta contains 28 once. Hence Delta is nonzero modulo L. Since `2 Delta in L`, its order is exactly two.

The same functional detects `T_alpha`, consistently with the three-reflection identity above.

## 6. Automorphism shortcut also fails

An exhaustive check over all 24 units modulo 70 and all 24 coordinate permutations finds no pair `(u,sigma)` with

    u * sigma(beta) = gamma mod 70.

Thus the missing correspondence is not the graph of an ordinary diagonal/permutation automorphism of the Fermat surface.

## 7. Consequences

This unifies several previous checkpoints:

- U42's unsquared parity obstruction;
- U43's rank-one motive `N tensor L^{-2}` of order two;
- U46's missing `R5`--`R7` correspondence;
- U39-U40's squared Jacobi-sum arithmetic.

They are manifestations of the same unresolved order-two motivic class.

The Otsubo-Yamazaki reflection and multiplication correspondences prove the square triviality but do not kill this class itself. Their discussion of the full Picard group of invertible Chow motives is conditional on conjectural inputs in the relevant classification direction, so one cannot conclude unconditionally that an order-two realization is an Artin/Tate motive.

## 8. Research direction

Any successful construction must supply one genuinely new algebraic relation that is odd under epsilon. Producing additional even relations, deeper Davenport-Hasse combinations, or another tensor square cannot solve the remaining block.

Promising sources of an odd relation include:

1. a characteristic-p algebraic Tate cycle with a provable characteristic-zero lift;
2. a genuinely non-toric complete-intersection or non-complete-intersection cycle detected by Favero-Kelly's formula;
3. a coupled matrix factorization whose primitive Chern character is odd under epsilon;
4. a mixed Horikawa correspondence not generated by the classical Jacobi-sum identities.
