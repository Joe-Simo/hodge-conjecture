# U30: paired torus surfaces on the degree-7 quotients pull back to classical linear cycles

Status: exact obstruction for the full monomial-pairing construction. This does not solve the Hodge conjecture.

Date: 2026-09-14.

## Candidate construction

For either degree-7 Delsarte quotient Y_A, write its defining polynomial as the sum of its six Delsarte monomials

    F = M_1+...+M_6.

For any perfect pairing of the six monomials, impose

    M_i+M_j=0

for each of the three pairs. Since the three equations sum to F=0, their torus locus is a surface in Y_A.

Exact rank computation shows that for all 15 perfect pairings, the three exponent-difference vectors have rank 3 for both quotient matrices. Thus all 15 pairings define genuine two-dimensional torus loci.

Several of these loci split into multiple torus components: the saturation indices of the exponent-difference lattices are

- for A2: index 1 for 8 pairings, index 2 for 6 pairings, index 4 for 1 pairing;
- for A3: index 1 for 12 pairings, index 2 for 3 pairings.

The unique index-4 pairing for A2 is

    (M1,M5), (M2,M3), (M4,M6).

So there are explicit torus-meeting algebraic surface components, apparently avoiding the boundary obstruction U29.

## Exact pullback obstruction

Let pi_B : X_70 -> Y_A be the standard Fermat-to-Delsarte monomial map with

    B=70 A^{-1}.

By construction

    A B = 70 I.

Therefore each defining Delsarte monomial pulls back to a pure Fermat monomial:

    pi_B^*(M_i) = z_i^70

(up to the fixed nonzero coefficients of the Delsarte equation).

Consequently every paired equation pulls back to

    z_i^70 + z_j^70 = 0.

Over C this factors into the 70 linear equations

    z_i = lambda z_j,  lambda^70=-1.

For three pairs, each torus surface therefore pulls back to a union of the classical linear P^2 cycles on the Fermat fourfold obtained by pairing the six coordinates.

## Consequence for alpha

The cohomology generated by these classical paired linear cycles has character support only on the corresponding complementary-pair sectors. U06/U13 established that

    alpha=(1,20,24,42,61,62)

contains no complementary coordinate pair modulo 70 and is not in the paired complete-intersection/linear-cycle sector.

Hence the pullback of every monomial-pairing surface has zero projection to V(alpha).

The splitting of a paired surface into index-2 or index-4 torus components does not evade this: after pullback, each component is still supported in the span of the same classical paired linear-cycle arrangement. No alpha certificate is obtained.

## Broader structural lesson

Any quotient cycle constructed solely by algebraic relations among the six defining Delsarte monomials M_i risks pulling back to relations among the pure powers z_i^70 and hence remaining inside previously known Fermat cycle sectors.

To hit alpha through the quotient, the cycle must use the quotient coordinates in a genuinely different way—not merely pair or otherwise combine the six defining monomials so that the pullback reduces to classical Fermat-coordinate pairings.
