# U34: first saturated toric surface ansatz carrying the target character

Status: explicit geometric candidate construction on the dense torus. Nonzero primitive cohomology projection is not yet proved. This does not solve the Hodge conjecture.

Date: 2026-09-14.

## Relation lattice

Start with the exact homogeneous target lift from U33

    r0=(1,20,24,-28,-9,-8).

Choose the two simplest independent homogeneous relations

    r1=(1,0,-1,0,0,0),
    r2=(0,1,-1,0,0,0).

The 3x6 relation matrix L with rows r0,r1,r2 has rank 3. The gcd of all its 3x3 minors is 1, so the lattice generated by these rows is saturated in its rational span. Hence the associated torus subvariety is connected rather than splitting because of lattice torsion.

Because

    alpha-r0=(0,0,0,70,70,70),

we have alpha == r0 mod 70. Therefore alpha lies in the relation lattice modulo 70, and the full diagonal stabilizer of the torus is contained in ker(alpha). This candidate passes the U32 stabilizer obstruction by construction.

## Explicit equations

The relations r1,r2 impose, with nonzero constants a,b,

    z0 = a z2,
    z1 = b z2.

On the torus chart z2=1, write

    z0=a,
    z1=b,
    z3=x,
    z4=y,
    z5=z.

The r0 equation becomes

    a b^20 = lambda x^28 y^9 z^8.

The Fermat equation becomes

    1+a^70+b^70+x^70+y^70+z^70=0.

Thus the candidate is explicitly represented on this chart by two equations in five torus variables:

    a b^20 - lambda x^28 y^9 z^8 = 0,
    1+a^70+b^70+x^70+y^70+z^70 = 0.

After accounting for projective scaling, the expected dimension is 2, as required for an algebraic surface in the Fermat fourfold.

## Why this is new relative to failed candidates

- It meets the dense torus; it is not supported on a coordinate boundary.
- Its relation lattice is saturated.
- Its stabilizer is compatible with alpha by construction.
- It is not defined by pairing Fermat monomials, so U30's reduction to classical paired P2 cycles does not apply immediately.
- The defining relation genuinely involves all six Fermat coordinates.

## Remaining load-bearing checks

These properties are necessary but not sufficient. The next steps are:

1. prove the projective closure has a two-dimensional irreducible component inside X_70 and audit boundary components;
2. determine whether varying lambda gives distinct cycles or only rationally equivalent ambient classes;
3. compute the primitive alpha-component of the cycle class, preferably through a residue/period formula or the Favero-Kelly Chern-character trace;
4. if the coefficient is nonzero, average over the Galois orbit to obtain the rational Hodge block and independently audit the complete implication.

The decisive next calculation is therefore no longer a stabilizer search: it is the alpha-period of this explicit saturated toric surface.
