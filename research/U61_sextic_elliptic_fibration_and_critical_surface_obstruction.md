# U61: the minimal sextic has non-isotrivial elliptic fibrations, but its first nonlinear critical surface is ambient

**Status:** the fibration, non-isotriviality and connected Kummer-cover calculation below are exact. They eliminate one natural nonlinear surface on the U60 sextic; they do not rule out all nonlinear surfaces and do not solve the Hodge conjecture.

Date: 2026-09-16. Base: U60.

## 1. The sextic and a multiplicity-three line

Use the U60 sextic

    F = M1+M2+M3+M4+M5+M6

with

    M1=y0^3 y1 y3^2,
    M2=y0^3 y2^2 y3,
    M3=y1 y2^2 y3^2 y4,
    M4=y0 y5^5,
    M5=y0^3 y1 y2^2,
    M6=y0 y1^2 y4^3.

The coordinate line

    L={y0=y1=y2=y5=0}

is contained with multiplicity three. Project from L. For a base point

    [a0:a1:a2:a5] in P^3

write a plane through L as

    y0=t a0, y1=t a1, y2=t a2, y5=t a5,
    y3=u, y4=v.

After dividing the restricted sextic by t^3, the residual plane cubic is

    G = a1 a2^2 u^2 v
      + a0 a1^2 v^3
      + t a0^3 a1 u^2
      + t^2 a0^3 a2^2 u
      + t^3 (a0 a5^5 + a0^3 a1 a2^2).                 (1.1)

It contains the rational point

    P=[u:v:t]=[1:0:0].

At P the gradient is

    (G_u,G_v,G_t)=(0, a1 a2^2, a0^3 a1),

so P is smooth over the dense base locus a0 a1 a2 !=0. Thus projection from L gives a genuine genus-one/elliptic fibration with section over a dense open subset of P^3.

The other three multiplicity-three coordinate lines give analogous genus-one fibrations.

## 2. None of the four fibrations is isotrivial

For (1.1), project the cubic from P. A line through P is parametrized by

    (v,t)=(r lambda,s lambda).

After removing the known factor lambda, the residual quadratic has branch binary quartic

    Q(r,s)=
      -4 a0 a1^3 a2^2 r^4
      -4 a0^4 a1^3 r^3 s
      -4 a0^3 a1^2 a2^4 r s^3
      -4 a0 a1 a2^2 a5^5 r s^3
      +(-4 a0^6 a1^2 a2^2 + a0^6 a2^4 -4 a0^4 a1 a5^5)s^4.

For a binary quartic ar^4+br^3s+cr^2s^2+drs^3+es^4 use

    I=12ae-3bd+c^2,
    J=72ace+9bcd-27ad^2-27b^2e-2c^3.

Two exact smooth fibers give

    (a0,a1,a2,a5)=(1,1,1,1):
       I=240, J=9936,
       J^2/I^3 = 14283/2000;

    (a0,a1,a2,a5)=(1,1,1,2):
       I=4704, J=1938384,
       J^2/I^3 = 11093787/307328.

Both quartic discriminants are nonzero, and the two ratios differ. Since the j-invariant is a nonconstant rational function of J^2/I^3, this elliptic fibration is non-isotrivial.

Repeating the same projection-from-the-section calculation for the other three multiplicity-three lines gives two smooth base fibers with different J^2/I^3 in every case. Therefore **all four singular-line elliptic fibrations are non-isotrivial**. A finite dominant base change cannot make a nonconstant j-function constant, so none becomes birationally a fixed elliptic curve times the base by this route.

## 3. The nonlinear critical surface

The critical locus of the first fibration on the dense torus is cut out by

    F=0,
    y3 F_y3=0,
    y4 F_y4=0.

In the six monomial coordinates this is the linear system

    M1+M2+M3+M4+M5+M6=0,
    2M1+M2+2M3=0,
    M3+3M6=0.

Put

    p=M4, q=M5, r=M6.

Then the corresponding plane in the monomial hyperplane is

    M1=p+q+4r,
    M2=-2(p+q+r),
    M3=-3r.                                             (3.1)

This is a genuine non-coordinate P^2 meeting the dense monomial torus.

The Delsarte monomial map from the sextic torus to its monomial hyperplane has degree

    |det A|/6 = 420/6 = 70.

The question is whether the preimage of the P^2 in (3.1) splits. If it split, individual components could have nontrivial character classes even though their sum were ambient.

## 4. Exact Kummer calculation: the P2 preimage is connected of degree 70

Work on y0=1. Removing the y0-column from the exponent matrix gives a one-dimensional left kernel generated by

    ell=(4,10,-9,0,-1,3).

Hence the six monomial values satisfy

    M1^4 M2^10 M6^3 = M3^9 M5.                         (4.1)

On the projective chart p !=0 write

    q=p Q, r=p R,
    S1=1+Q+4R,
    S2=1+Q+R.

Substituting (3.1) into (4.1) yields

    p^7 = c * R^6 Q / (S1^4 S2^10),                    (4.2)

for a nonzero constant c in C.

But p=M4=y5^5, so

    y5^35 = c * R^6 Q / (S1^4 S2^10).                  (4.3)

The right side has valuation 1 along Q=0. It is therefore neither a fifth nor a seventh power in C(Q,R). Since C contains all 35th roots of unity, Kummer theory gives

    [C(Q,R)(y5):C(Q,R)] = 35.

The remaining square root comes from y2. Solving the other monomial equations gives, modulo squares in C(Q,R)(y5),

    y2^2 ~ Q/S1.

The base element Q/S1 is not a square in C(Q,R), because its valuations at Q=0 and S1=0 are odd. It cannot become a square in an odd-degree extension of degree 35: if a base-field element becomes a square in an odd-degree finite extension, its square class was already trivial (equivalently use the norm on square classes).

Therefore adjoining y2 contributes another factor 2, and the restricted function-field degree is

    35*2=70.

So the P^2 preimage is generically connected/irreducible and has the full degree of the ambient monomial map. The repeated branch line M3=0=M6 has **not** caused a splitting.

## 5. Consequence for the target class

On the dense torus, the critical surface is the entire pullback of the linear P^2 in the monomial hyperplane. Its total cycle class is therefore generated by the two divisor classes defining that linear section. It has no primitive target component.

Upon projective closure there can be additional boundary components. Those are supported on coordinate boundary strata; by the preserved boundary-character obstruction they have zero projection to the nonzero-coordinate target character. Since the dense component is irreducible, subtracting boundary components cannot reveal a hidden target-bearing dense component.

Thus **the most natural nonlinear surface supplied by the sextic elliptic fibration does not carry the U60 target orbit.**

## 6. Research consequence

U60 remains a useful minimal-degree reformulation, but three immediate shortcuts are now closed:

- no Esser determinant/quadric-bundle rationality;
- no isotrivial elliptic fibration after finite base change;
- no splitting of the first non-coordinate discriminant/critical surface into target-bearing components.

The next sextic-side search should use surfaces not obtained as complete pullbacks of linear subspaces under the degree-70 monomial map—e.g. nonlinear multisections, Mordell-Weil constructions, or components arising from special reducible fibers.

The calculations are exact and self-reviewed, not formally verified or independently expert-reviewed.
