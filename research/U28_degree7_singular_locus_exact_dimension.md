# U28: exact singular-locus dimension of the two degree-7 quotients

Status: exact symbolic computation for the two U25 quotient equations. This does not prove algebraicity and does not solve the Hodge conjecture.

Date: 2026-09-14.

## Equations

For the two transposed U23 exponent matrices A2 and A3, the corresponding ordinary degree-7 Delsarte fourfold equations in P^5 are:

A2:

    f2 = x0^4*x4^2*x5
       + x0*x1^5*x5
       + x0*x1^2*x2^3*x4
       + x0*x2*x3^4*x4
       + x2^3*x3*x4*x5^2
       + x3^2*x4^2*x5^3.

A3:

    f3 = x0^4*x2*x5^2
       + x0*x1^5*x4
       + x0*x1^2*x2^2*x4*x5
       + x0*x2*x3^4*x5
       + x2^2*x3*x4^3*x5
       + x2*x3^2*x4^2*x5^2.

Because the exponent matrices are invertible, the logarithmic gradient equations on the dense torus force the vector of monomial values to vanish, which is impossible there. Hence all singular points lie on the coordinate boundary.

## Coordinate-stratum audit

A coordinate projective subspace obtained by setting variables indexed by Z to zero is contained identically in the singular locus exactly when every monomial has total exponent at least 2 along Z. Exact enumeration gives the minimal such Z.

For A2, minimal Z are

    {x0,x2,x5}, {x0,x4,x5}, {x1,x2,x4}, {x1,x3,x4},
    {x0,x1,x2,x3}, {x0,x1,x3,x5}.

These give four projective planes and two projective lines contained in Sing(Y2).

For A3, minimal Z are

    {x0,x2,x4}, {x0,x3,x4}, {x0,x4,x5}, {x1,x2,x5}, {x1,x3,x5},
    {x0,x1,x2,x3}.

These give five projective planes and one projective line contained in Sing(Y3).

## Gröbner-basis dimension check

To exclude hidden higher-dimensional components caused by cancellations, compute the homogeneous Jacobian ideals

    J_i = (partial f_i/partial x0, ..., partial f_i/partial x5)

with exact rational/integer arithmetic and a graded reverse lexicographic Gröbner basis.

For f2 the basis has 59 elements; for f3 it has 32 elements. In each case the leading-monomial ideal has height 3 (minimum vertex-cover size 3 on the supports of the leading monomials), hence the affine Jacobian cone has Krull dimension

    6 - 3 = 3.

Therefore the projective singular locus has dimension exactly

    3 - 1 = 2

for both Y2 and Y3.

This independently agrees with the maximal coordinate planes found above and rules out any hidden 3-dimensional projective singular component.

## Interpretation

The quotients are singular along surfaces, not merely isolated points or curves. Resolving them will introduce algebraic exceptional strata, but this alone does not prove the target Hodge class algebraic: U25 shows that the quotient also carries a genuine 24-dimensional primitive rational Hodge block, exactly the alpha orbit, which need not be supported on exceptional strata.

Thus the singular-resolution shortcut is not automatic. A successful continuation would need either:

1. a decomposition/intersection-cohomology argument showing that this entire primitive block is generated by resolution strata; or
2. an explicit algebraic surface on the quotient/resolution whose class has nonzero projection to the unique primitive Hodge orbit.
