# U39 exact-source audit

Checked 2026-09-15, against U36 remote base 467188a and local U38. This is a source/application audit, not a global novelty review. Web primary texts were inspected; source PDFs are not redistributed in the evidence archive.

1. Noriyuki Otsubo, *Hypergeometric functions over finite fields*, arXiv:2108.06754v5, 1 August2023. The arXiv version history was checked. PDF printed pages3 and10 were read as screenshots as well as parsed text. Definition2.1 uses g=-G; Proposition2.2(iii) gives reflection, and Theorem3.10 gives multiplication. Translation to positive Gauss sums supplies the inverse exponent -na. All characters divided by in the 35-term certificate are nontrivial. Exact source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.06754v5

2. Tetsuji Shioda and Toshiyuki Katsura, *On Fermat varieties*, Tohoku Mathematical Journal31(1979),97–115; DOI10.2748/tmj/1178229881. Printed page107 / PDF page11 was read directly in screenshot. Equations(3.1)–(3.4) give the even-dimensional plus normalization; here dimension4 means sign+1. The finite field is the residue field of K at a good prime, whose size is the least power of the rational prime congruent1 mod70. Source: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/tmj1949/31/1/31_1_97/_pdf

3. J.S.Milne, *Algebraic Number Theory*, version3.08 (19 July2020). Theorem6.4 supplies cyclotomic ramification; Proposition7.55 supplies the local Eisenstein/total-ramification criterion; Theorem8.31 supplies Chebotarev density. The proof uses density for all primes of K away from a finite set, not just the five or eight sampled rational primes. Source: https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/ANT.pdf

4. J.S.Milne, *Fields and Galois Theory*, author-hosted copy inspected this date. Printed pages74–76, Propositions5.27–5.28 and Theorem5.30: Kummer character, degree, and radicand-equivalence conditions. Application: K contains mu70, D is a square, and 23 is coprime to35. Source: https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/FT.pdf

5. N.Otsubo and T.Yamazaki, *Motivic Gauss and Jacobi sums*, arXiv:2402.06072v3, posted2 March2025; published in Annales de la Faculte des sciences de Toulouse, serie6,35(2026),95–135, DOI10.5802/afst.1842. Checked the publisher record and pinned preprint. Theorem1.2 and Remark6.6 explicitly identify Frobenius on the character eigenspace. Use d=70,n=5,c=-1 and exponents(20,24,42,61,62), with nontrivial product exponent69. The covariant-motive convention may dualize cohomological characters, which does not change fixed fields. Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.06072v3 ; publisher record: https://afst.centre-mersenne.org/articles/10.5802/afst.1842/

Additional research lead, not an input to the cycle-field theorem: source5 Theorem7.2 proves a multiplication isomorphism for Fermat Chow motives over fields containing mu_d, including characteristic zero. Translating this particular Gauss certificate into explicit characteristic-zero correspondences still requires a separate dictionary and control of the finite-order defect. Neither its finite-field Gauss statement nor the conditional discussion of Beilinson/Tate in Section8 proves algebraicity of our class. This source therefore supplies a possible next constructive route, not a missing conclusion to import silently.
